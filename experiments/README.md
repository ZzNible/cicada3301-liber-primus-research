## Reading negative results correctly

`REJECTED` is always scoped. A failed implementation does not automatically reject every member of a broad family.

Similarly, `OPEN` does not mean supported. It means the available evidence does not decide the claim.

## Reproduction

Where raw third-party artifacts cannot responsibly be redistributed, these summaries provide hashes, fixed statistics, experiment IDs and the exact conclusions needed to locate or independently reconstruct the tests from provenance-pinned public material.

Future public releases may promote complete experiment packages when their inputs are redistributable and their provenance/rights are sufficiently clear.
