# Negative Results Worth Not Repeating

`REJECTED` always means rejected **within the stated scope**, not disproven in every imaginable form.

## LP2 cipher/mechanism families

Do not reopen without genuinely new independent evidence:

- Caesar or fixed substitution by page based on `3:5:8`;
- direct English extraction from the 55 `3:5:8` runes;
- period-55 Vigenere;
- affine `181/450` traversal;
- direct pad `[39:135]` using item 4;
- previously tested local feedback/autokey constructions;
- short Vigenere, Caesar or simple affine families;
- periods `5`, `19`, or `1279`;
- simple Fibonacci keystream;
- naive word-to-word addressing;
- direct folding of the two `DJU-BEI-AE` occurrences;
- literal uncontrolled interpretations of `shed your circumference`.

## Image / steganographic families

- OutGuess default-key extraction producing 58,152 bytes: not supported as a useful route in the tested form.
- T13 subpixel A/B channel: rejected within tested scope.
- JPEG 9/10-pixel grid channel: rejected within tested scope.
- Several tree/image convolution families produced visually interesting but non-predictive fits; community parameter guesses must not be promoted to Cicada-authenticated mechanisms.

## Low-repeat signal: rejected explanations or dependent effects

- material transcription/page-assignment error as explanation of the 86 repeats;
- punctuation/layout-marker proximity as substantial explanation;
- line/page resets;
- rubricated-runic regions;
- lag-2 anti-recurrence beyond fitted first-order structure;
- stable 29-rank predictor improving held-out LP2;
- all-29 diagonal minima as substantial independent evidence beyond `K=86`;
- temporal self-repeat floor as substantial independent evidence beyond `K=86`;
- pure no-repeat generator;
- simple uniform max-run-2 generator;
- fitted soft-repeat parameter as independent mechanism evidence.

Important caveat: complete state-independence has **not** been established. One state-allocation closure test lacked sufficient positive-control power.

## Source-derived mechanism searches

**REJECTED scoped:** preserved authenticated/pinned 2012–2014 source material independently fixes an immediate-state, previous-symbol, feedback or anti-repeat mechanism suitable for reopening LP2 fitting.

Community solver implementations of autokey/previous-symbol ideas do not count as Cicada-authenticated evidence.

## CicadaOS / cross-round misuse

- Authentic CicadaOS provenance alone does not license broad LP2 byte sweeps.
- Preserved 2014 sources did not deterministically prescribe CicadaOS bytes as LP2 input in the bounded audit.
- Marker `761` alone does not select `761.mp3` as a byte source.

Cross-round semantic reuse itself remains established; the rejected claim is the unjustified jump from semantic reuse to arbitrary byte reuse.

## AN END

Do not brute-force combinations of:

- SHA variants;
- URL vs HTML/body/file;
- charset/newline normalization;
- decompression;
- headers;
- rendered representations;
- arbitrary hidden-service locators.

The solved text establishes page-to-hash semantics, but not the algorithm or serialization.

Also rejected within scope:

- deterministic full Freenet CHK from the 64-byte target alone;
- public DUTA as deterministic historical-content custody;
- Common Crawl/onion.to as deterministic hidden-service-origin content custody;
- repeated broad public archive enumeration as a way to manufacture another custody route.

## Why publish this list?

Negative results are a major community asset. They reduce duplicated effort and make genuinely new evidence easier to recognize.
